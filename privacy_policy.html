Gizlilik Politikasi

Son guncelleme: 16 Subat 2026

Bu politika, VetDose uygulamasinda verilerin nasil islendigi ve hangi verilerin ucuncu taraf hizmetlere iletildigi konusunda bilgi verir.

1. Toplanan ve Iletilen Veriler

VetDose, cekirdek medikal kullanimda ad/e-posta/telefon gibi dogrudan kimlik verilerini toplamaz.

Abonelik ve satin alma dogrulamasi icin asagidaki sinirli veriler iletilir:

  • Abonelik/satin alma bilgisi: Pro erisimi dogrulamak icin.
  • Anonim uygulama kullanici tanimlayicisi: RevenueCat tarafindan entitlement yonetimi icin olusturulabilir.

Toplanmayan veriler:

  • Ad, e-posta, telefon, fiziksel adres
  • Konum verisi
  • Reklam izleme verisi veya ucuncu taraf reklam profilleme verisi
  • Kendi sunucularimiza gonderilen analytics olaylari

2. Cihazda Yerel Saklanan Veriler

Asagidaki veriler temel olarak cihazda yerel saklanir:

  • Bolge/icerik tercihi: Turkiye veya uluslararasi mod secimi
  • Ilac veritabani: Ilac ve dozaj icerikleri
  • Abonelik cache bilgisi: Uygulama deneyimini hizlandirmak icin gecici abonelik durumu

3. Ucuncu Taraf Hizmetler

  • RevenueCat: Uygulama ici satin alma ve abonelik dogrulamasi icin kullanilir. RevenueCat Gizlilik Politikasi
  • Apple App Store: Odeme islemleri Apple tarafindan yonetilir. Apple Gizlilik Politikasi

4. Veri Saklama ve Silme

Uygulama icindeki yerel veriler cihazinizda saklanir; uygulamayi kaldirdiginizda silinir.

Abonelik/satin alma ile ilgili kayitlar Apple ve RevenueCat tarafinda kendi saklama politikalarina gore tutulabilir.

Veri talepleri icin bize ulasabilirsiniz: vetdoseapp@gmail.com

5. Cocuklarin Gizliligi

VetDose, 13 yas altina yonelik tasarlanmamistir ve bilerek bu gruptan veri toplamaz.

6. Guvenlik

Veri iletimi, kullanilan platform servislerinin guvenli iletim mekanizmalari uzerinden yapilir. Tedavi karariyla ilgili medikal icerikler referans amaclidir.

7. Sorumluluk Reddi

Uygulamadaki ilac/doz bilgileri referans amaclidir; klinik karar sorumlulugu lisansli veteriner hekime aittir.

8. Politika Degisiklikleri

Bu politika zaman icinde guncellenebilir. Guncel surum bu sayfada yayinlanir.

9. Iletisim

E-posta: vetdoseapp@gmail.com

10. KVKK Uyumu (6698 Sayili Kanun)

VetDose, 6698 sayili Kisisel Verilerin Korunmasi Kanunu (“KVKK”) kapsaminda kisisel veri isleme ilkelerine uygun hareket eder.

Veri Sorumlusu: VetDose uygulamasi — vetdoseapp@gmail.com

Islenen kisisel veriler:

  • Anonim cihaz/uygulama tanimlayicisi (RevenueCat abonelik dogrulamasi icin)
  • Bolge/dil tercihi (yerel cihazda sakli)

Isleme amaci: Abonelik dogrulamasi, uygulama icerigi kisisellestirme (bolge secimi).

Hukuki dayanak: Acik riza (abonelik islemleri), sozlesmenin ifasi (uygulama hizmeti).

KVKK Madde 11 kapsamindaki haklariniz:

  • Kisisel verilerinizin islenip islenmedigini ogrenme
  • Kisisel verileriniz islenmisse buna iliskin bilgi talep etme
  • Kisisel verilerinizin islenme amacini ve bunlarin amacina uygun kullanilip kullanilmadigini ogrenme
  • Yurt icinde veya yurt disinda kisisel verilerin aktarildigi ucuncu kisileri bilme
  • Kisisel verilerin eksik veya yanlis islenmis olmasi halinde bunlarin duzeltilmesini isteme
  • KVKK m.7 kapsaminda kisisel verilerin silinmesini veya yok edilmesini isteme
  • Islenen verilerin munhasiran otomatik sistemler vasitasiyla analiz edilmesi suretiyle kisiniz aleyhine bir sonucun ortaya cikmasina itiraz etme
  • Kisisel verilerin kanuna aykiri olarak islenmesi sebebiyle zarara ugramaniz halinde zararin giderilmesini talep etme

Taleplerinizi vetdoseapp@gmail.com adresine iletebilirsiniz.

Ayrica Kisisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu'na basvuru hakkiniz saklidir.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

This policy explains which data VetDose processes and what limited data is transmitted to third-party services.

1. Data We Collect and Transmit

VetDose does not collect direct personal identity data such as name, email, or phone number for core medical use.

For subscriptions and purchase validation, the following limited data may be transmitted:

  • Purchase/subscription information: To validate Pro access.
  • Anonymous app user identifier: May be generated by RevenueCat for entitlement management.

Data not collected:

  • Name, email, phone number, physical address
  • Location data
  • Advertising tracking/profile data
  • Analytics events sent to our own backend servers

2. Data Stored Locally on Device

The following data is primarily stored locally on your device:

  • Region/content preference: Turkey or international mode selection
  • Drug database: Drug and dosage content
  • Subscription cache: Temporary local subscription state for app experience

3. Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat: Used for in-app purchase and subscription validation. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: Payment processing is managed by Apple. Apple Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Subscription and purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own retention policies.

For data questions/requests, contact: vetdoseapp@gmail.com

5. Children's Privacy

VetDose is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from that age group.

6. Security

Data transmission uses the security mechanisms of the underlying platform services. Medical content is provided for reference support.

7. Disclaimer

Drug and dosage information is reference-only; final treatment decisions remain the responsibility of a licensed veterinarian.

8. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated over time. The current version is always published on this page.

9. Contact

Email: vetdoseapp@gmail.com

10. Data Protection Rights (GDPR / KVKK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or Turkey, you have certain data protection rights under the GDPR and/or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”).

Data Controller: VetDose App — vetdoseapp@gmail.com

Personal data processed:

  • Anonymous device/app identifier (for RevenueCat subscription validation)
  • Region/language preference (stored locally on device)

Legal basis: Explicit consent (subscription transactions), performance of contract (app service delivery).

Your rights include:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Right to restrict or object to processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right not to be subject to automated individual decision-making
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise your rights, contact us at vetdoseapp@gmail.com.

For Turkish residents, you may also file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).
