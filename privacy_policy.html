Gizlilik Politikasi

Son guncelleme: Haziran 2025

VetDose uygulamasini kullandiginiz icin tesekkur ederiz. Gizliliginiz bizim icin onemlidir. Bu politika, uygulamamizin verilerinizi nasil isledigini aciklar.

1. Toplanan Veriler

VetDose hicbir kisisel veri toplamaz.

Uygulamamiz:

  • Ad, e-posta, telefon numarasi gibi kisisel bilgiler toplamaz
  • Konum verisi toplamaz
  • Cihaz tanimlayicilari toplamaz
  • Kullanim analizleri (analytics) gondermez
  • Ucuncu taraf reklamlari gostermez

2. Yerel Olarak Saklanan Veriler

Asagidaki veriler yalnizca cihazinizda yerel olarak saklanir ve hicbir sunucuya gonderilmez:

  • Konum tercihi: Turkiye veya Uluslararasi mod secimi
  • Ilac veritabani: Tum ilac ve dozaj verileri cihazda yerel olarak calisikullanir
  • Abonelik durumu: Pro abonelik bilgisi

3. Ucuncu Taraf Hizmetleri

VetDose asagidaki ucuncu taraf hizmetini kullanir:

  • RevenueCat: Uygulama ici satin alma ve abonelik yonetimi icin kullanilir. RevenueCat, anonim bir kullanici kimligi ile yalnizca abonelik durumunu isler. Kisisel bilgilerinize erismez. RevenueCat Gizlilik Politikasi
  • Apple App Store: Odeme islemleri Apple tarafindan yonetilir. Apple Gizlilik Politikasi

4. Cocuklarin Gizliligi

VetDose, 13 yasindan kucuk cocuklardan bilerek veri toplamaz. Uygulama profesyonel veteriner hekimler ve ogrenciler icin tasarlanmistir.

5. Veri Guvenligi

Tum ilac verileri cihazinizda yerel olarak saklanir. Sunucu tarafli veri depolama veya aktarimi yoktur. Abonelik islemleri Apple'in guvenli odeme altyapisi uzerinden gerceklesir.

6. Sorumluluk Reddi

VetDose yalnizca referans amaclidir. Uygulamadaki ilac dozaj bilgileri tedavi karari yerine gecmez. Tedavi kararlari her zaman veteriner hekimin sorumlulugundadir.

7. Degisiklikler

Bu gizlilik politikasi zaman zaman guncellenebilir. Onemli degisiklikler uygulama icinden bildirilecektir.

8. Iletisim

Sorulariniz icin bize ulasabilirsiniz:

E-posta: vetdoseapp@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Thank you for using VetDose. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how our application handles your data.

1. Data Collection

VetDose does not collect any personal data.

Our application:

  • Does not collect personal information such as name, email, or phone number
  • Does not collect location data
  • Does not collect device identifiers
  • Does not send usage analytics
  • Does not display third-party advertisements

2. Locally Stored Data

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to any server:

  • Location preference: Turkey or International mode selection
  • Drug database: All drug and dosage data operates locally on the device
  • Subscription status: Pro subscription information

3. Third-Party Services

VetDose uses the following third-party service:

  • RevenueCat: Used for in-app purchase and subscription management. RevenueCat processes only subscription status with an anonymous user identifier. It does not access your personal information. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: Payment transactions are managed by Apple. Apple Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy

VetDose does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The application is designed for professional veterinarians and students.

5. Data Security

All drug data is stored locally on your device. There is no server-side data storage or transmission. Subscription transactions are processed through Apple's secure payment infrastructure.

6. Disclaimer

VetDose is for reference purposes only. Drug dosage information in the application does not substitute for treatment decisions. Treatment decisions are always the responsibility of the attending veterinarian.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the application.
